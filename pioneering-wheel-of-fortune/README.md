# Pioneering Wheel of Fortune

A single-file, offline Wheel of Fortune game for the Pioneering Adventure Skill, for the 142nd Dublin (Esker) Scout Group. It works the same way as the Air Adventure Stage 3 game, in its own folder.

## Running it

Double-click `index.html` in Chrome or Edge. Open the projector window from the Scouter panel and drag it to the projector. See `../aviation-wheel-of-fortune/README.md` for the full turn-by-turn steps, which are identical.

## What is in the bank

- 52 questions across 10 areas, mapped to the Pioneering criteria for Stages 1 to 5: ropes, knots, lashings and poles, safety and first aid, tools, elastic band gadgets, structures and guy ropes, pulleys and lifting, Leave No Trace, and teamwork.
- 17 phrases (easy, medium and hard).
- These are general pioneering knowledge written to the criteria. They have not been checked against the Scouting Ireland badge book.

## Saved data

This game stores its progress under `pioneer.*` keys, so it does not overwrite the Air Adventure game's saved scores when both are open in the same browser.
